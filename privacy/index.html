Privacy Policy

Monero Wallet · Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Monero Wallet mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet for Monero (XMR). We built it with privacy as a core principle: we do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

In short: your wallet, keys and seed phrase stay on your device. We do not have access to them, and we cannot recover them for you.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • We do not collect, store or transmit your private keys, view keys, spend keys, seed phrase (mnemonic) or wallet password.
  • We do not run our own analytics or advertising trackers inside the App.
  • We do not keep records of your balances, addresses or transaction history on our servers.

2. Information stored on your device

To work as a wallet, the App stores the following data locally on your device only, in encrypted form where supported by your device:

  • Your wallet files, keys and seed phrase
  • Your wallet password or PIN settings, and biometric unlock preference (biometric data itself is handled by your device's operating system and is never accessible to the App)
  • Your address book, transaction notes and App settings

This data is removed when you delete the wallet or uninstall the App. Please back up your seed phrase securely; if you lose it, nobody, including us, can restore your funds.

3. Network connections

To sync your wallet and send transactions, the App connects to Monero network nodes. When it does:

  • The node you connect to (a public remote node, or your own node if you configure one) can see your device's IP address, in the same way any website can see the IP address of a visitor.
  • Because of how Monero works, a remote node cannot see your balance or link your transactions to your addresses, but it may see when your wallet is syncing.
  • For maximum privacy, you can connect the App to your own node or use a VPN or Tor on your device.

If the App displays fiat price information, it may request exchange-rate data from a third-party price provider. These requests do not include your wallet data.

4. Device permissions

  • Internet: to connect to the Monero network.
  • Camera (optional): only to scan QR codes for addresses. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded.
  • Biometrics (optional): to unlock the App using your device's fingerprint or face unlock.
  • Notifications (optional): to alert you about incoming transactions or sync status.

5. Third-party services

The App is distributed through app stores such as Google Play. Those platforms may collect information, such as crash reports and install data, under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, because we do not collect it in the first place.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect data stored on your device. However, the security of your wallet also depends on you: keep your device protected with a screen lock, never share your seed phrase, and only install the App from official sources.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Your rights

Since we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You remain in full control of the data on your device and can delete it at any time by removing the wallet or uninstalling the App.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by opening an issue at github.com/Abubakar2525-dev/monero-wallet/issues.
