Privacy Policy — Monero Wallet for Android

Last updated: December 22, 2025

Important notice. This is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Monero Project, Monero Core Team, or any official Monero organization.

1. Summary

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app is designed with privacy as a core principle:

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not require account registration
  • We do not have access to your funds or keys
  • We do not track your usage or behavior

2. Data stored locally on your device

The app stores the following data exclusively on your device.

Sensitive data (encrypted with the Android Keystore)

  • Seed phrases (mnemonic recovery words)
  • Private spend keys
  • Private view keys
  • The random per-wallet password that protects the on-device wallet files

App data (local storage)

  • Wallet names and addresses
  • Transaction history (fetched from the network and cached by the wallet engine)
  • Sync state and preferences
  • Invoice records (if you use the merchant features)
  • App settings (appearance, language, currency, notifications, daemon list, and so on)

We never have access to this data. It remains on your device and under your control. The app sets android:allowBackup="false", so Android does not copy wallet data to cloud backups.

3. Network communications

The app communicates with external services to function.

Monero network nodes (daemons)

  • Purpose: synchronize wallet balance and broadcast transactions
  • Data sent: blockchain RPC requests and transaction broadcast data
  • Privacy note: node operators can see your IP address and request patterns. Your seed and private keys are never sent. The wallet scans blocks locally. Turn on Use Tor in Settings → Privacy to route the node connection through the Tor client built into the app, or point the app at your own node.

Price data services

  • Purpose: display cryptocurrency prices in your local currency
  • Data sent: standard HTTP requests (CoinGecko, with Coinpaprika as a fallback)
  • Privacy note: no wallet or personal data is transmitted. These requests are made directly, not through Tor.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Location data
  • Contact lists or photos
  • Any information about your transactions or balances

5. Third-party services

The app may connect to third-party services:

  • Daemon nodes: public Monero nodes (policies vary by operator), or a node you enter yourself
  • Price data: CoinGecko API (see coingecko.com/privacy), Coinpaprika as a fallback

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you connect to.

6. Camera and biometric authentication

  • Camera: used only when you tap Scan to read a QR code. Frames are decoded on the device (ML Kit bundled model and ZXing) and are never stored or uploaded.
  • Biometrics: if enabled, the app uses Android's biometric prompt. Biometric data is processed by Android, not by the app. We never receive or store biometric data.

7. Notifications

If you enable notifications:

  • Notifications are generated locally on your device
  • We do not use push notification servers
  • No data is sent to external servers for notifications

8. Data retention

  • Local data persists until you delete wallets or uninstall the app
  • Android removes the app's data when you uninstall it
  • You can delete all data by removing wallets in Settings

9. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • Delete wallets at any time (make sure you have seed phrase backups)
  • Change network servers to ones you trust or operate yourself
  • Disable biometric authentication in Settings
  • Use the built-in Tor client for enhanced IP privacy
  • Export your seed phrase for use in other wallets

10. Children's privacy

This app is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. International users

The app can be used globally. Data remains on your device and is not transferred to external servers under our control.

12. Security

We implement security best practices:

  • Sensitive data encrypted using the Android Keystore
  • No plaintext storage of secrets
  • HTTPS for price data requests (public daemon nodes may use plain HTTP)

However, no system is perfectly secure. Keep your device updated and protected.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect changes. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

14. Contact us

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us through the Play Store listing or the support channels provided in the app.
